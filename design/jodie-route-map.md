# Jodie Route Map — Filtration to Medical

## Main spine

1. **Filtration 123**
   - Brooks
   - failing gauge / O-ring assignment
   - establish Jodie attributes
   - first trust changes

2. **Sail Rigging**
   - introduce the sailors' working culture
   - establish "ropes of life"
   - first persistent knowledge flag

3. **Living Quarters**
   - Copperfield / Houdini encounter
   - truth, concealment, and social-navigation choices

4. **Water Storage**
   - dangerous timed crossing
   - tension-forward sequence
   - prior knowledge may alter options or interpretation

5. **Agro**
   - Jodie's fall
   - Tyler's cover story
   - Tyler relationship state begins to matter

6. **Distribution**
   - honey descent and cleanup
   - disguise / jumpsuit opportunity
   - room for a short earned branch

7. **Elevator Shaft**
   - rapid vertical movement through unnamed levels
   - prior state shapes how Jodie handles the descent

8. **Medical Intake**
   - end of the portfolio slice
   - earlier choices pay off in dialogue, trust, knowledge, and available responses

## Structural goal

All versions of Jodie can reach Medical Intake, but they should not arrive there as the same person.

Later development may introduce selective temporary branches that reconverge before or at Medical.

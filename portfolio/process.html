Development case study

From prose
to playable.

The same Tower journey designed for two different forms of storytelling.

In the prose

Jodie crosses Sail Rigging as part of a fixed sequence. The reader observes the route, the technical infrastructure, and the social rules that constrain her movement. Momentum comes from selected action, atmosphere and dramatic pacing.

In the Ink adaptation

Rigging becomes a set of decisions: ask what the “rope of life” means, help workers pull, test an unfamiliar control, or keep moving. Each choice reveals a different aspect of Jodie while influencing subsequent scenes.

KNOWLEDGE

Questions become options

Observing or handling equipment builds understanding that can later unlock warnings, routes and technical actions.

CAUSALITY

The Tower remembers

Interfering with a control can destabilize systems downstream. Preparation can protect Jodie when a later hazard calls for it.

CHARACTER

A route, many identities

Runs converge on Medical Intake, but Jodie's reputation, competence, connections and institutional visibility differ.

Design principle

The player is not primarily choosing where the story goes. The player shapes who Jodie becomes while she gets there. Stage 1 ends with an emerging profile and a preview of how those outcomes might affect the planned Stage 2.

What is implemented

Branching Ink choices, conditional text, route variation, accumulated knowledge and reputation, hazards, mitigation, delayed consequences, replay and optional developer diagnostics. Stage 2 gameplay is not yet included.

Workflow

Original prose and story architecture; Ink scripting and Inky-based testing; GitHub version control; automated multi-route QA with inkjs; AI-assisted implementation and review. The detailed treatment and interactive source remain separate portfolio artifacts.
